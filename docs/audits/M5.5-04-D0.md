## 4. Parked - two readings each, and what would settle them

Round 5 found two places where rules the original already held can point to different domains.\
Neither is introduced by this conversion, and no wording of this charter alone can settle them, because each is a question about how the domain axis composes with the work-type axis.

**P-1.\
Pinned against altered surface.**\
A pinned work-type's domain is a constant of the type; the tie-break says the domain is the surface the work alters.\
Reading A: the pin wins, and pinned work that alters another surface is mis-typed.\
Reading B: the altered surface wins, and the pin is a default.\
*Settles it:* whether any pinned work-type's real work ever alters a surface other than its pin - an observation over executed work.

**P-2.\
Incident surface against altered surface.**\
An incident's domain is the surface it hit; the fix's domain is the surface it alters.\
Reading A: they are two units of work, recovery on the hit surface and the fix on the altered one.\
Reading B: one unit, placed by the fix.\
*Settles it:* the incident posture note in `W0`, which the charter cites and does not restate.

Both belong with `B24`, the compositional split of the axes, which comes due at `W0`.

## 7. Director ruling and its application

**Ruled: option B, a seventh domain.**\
With it a principle wider than this gap: domains, work-types and traits are under-defined collections, and when a gap is determined it is on us to expand them for coverage.

### The new member

[`D7`](../../domains/D7-product-data.md) `product-data` - the product's own stored data: its records and the schema they are held in, as they exist live.\
Evidence: the records before and after, migration and repair logs, row counts and integrity checks, backups and restore points.\
Authored from `D1` and `D2` as exemplars; no tool names in its subject surface.\
It cites `A1` - structure changes only through a declared refactor, which a schema migration is - and `A9` - a change promoted to production is proven under interruption first.\
Both were read before citing.

### Which work-types reach it, decided per entry

Each candidate was judged on its body and its `evidenceContract`, against the placement rule: the domain is the surface the work alters.

| Work-type | Admitted | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `fix-a-bug-or-repair` | yes | a record repair is a repair; repro, fix and regression check all resolve against records |
| `retire-or-hard-cut` | yes | retiring a table or dataset is deleting a surface, with a reference scan |
| `publish-deploy-or-canonicalize` | yes | applying a landed migration to production is a deploy; deploy log and rollback anchor map to the migration log and restore point |
| `verify-gate-reactive`, `audit-a-surface`, `adversarial-design-review-upstream`, `design-a-contract-or-invariant` | yes | already free across every domain; data is a surface each can act on |
| `run-a-live-probe-or-smoke` | yes | observing live records at a revision is a live probe |
| `build-a-slice`, `merge-and-land` | no | their evidence is merged code; a migration's code is `delivery-code` |
| `validate-locally`, `author-guard-or-falsifier-tests` | no | they alter code and tests, not live records; a dry run against a copy is validating the migration's code |
| `meta-validate-dogfood`, `reset-or-converge-the-fleet` | no | neither acts on stored data; the fleet is live seats, which is `distribution` |
| every pinned type | no | pinned types name their own surface |

Eight work-types gained `product-data`.\
`D7`'s own `freeForTypes` lists exactly those eight, measured, so the new member adds nothing to the doubly-declared fault.
