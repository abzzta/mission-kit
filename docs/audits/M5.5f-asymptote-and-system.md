## 1. The rulings this states

- **Q-A**: axioms bind systems; a change answers to every system it alters - the one it modifies, every system containing it, and any system whose contract with it the change alters; an undeclared boundary means both bind.
- **Asymptote**: the mandate states a limit; no change may move a system away from it, and each change moves toward it as far as its scope allows.
- **Within the fence**: inside the scope, build at or nearer the limit; outside it, record the gap with a revival trigger and leave selection to the director.
- **`system` is an entity.**
