**Confirmed, and confirmed by the original's own evaluators.**\
Two original-version evaluators noted unprompted that the charter's trigger - *which role may attest, approve or decide* - is not answered by its body.\
The territory is the answer to that.

**Twelve findings; all corrected.**\
Five were pre-existing defects in the original, four of them raised unanimously.\
Seven were errors in the author's additions, all in territory and composition - the two concerns this conversion authored:

- the territory said an organisation of one *can hold every other position*, which includes the director's two;
- it said the organisation *has no second actor* while sending checks to the director - corrected to no second **agent**, with the director as the independent identity checks fall to;
- it said *every position has a role* beside its own definition of a gap, so it read as reporting none;
- it described the fallback two ways;
- it said authority comes from `role x work-type` in one place and `role x work-type x domain` in another;
- it cited "the next section" for a point made in the previous one;
- it cited this corpus's own backlog row from a charter. A charter is product read by every project, so it now cites the gap `T0` records rather than a row in mission-kit's own instance.

Full detail in `eval-R0/SCORES.md`.
