# Methodology - the how-you-operate layer

## Purpose

Ways of working.\
Named procedures for conducting work, each one declinable in a given situation and none of them optional once you are in the situation it names.

A methodology entry governs **conduct**: the sequence you follow, the independence you require, the evidence you demand before you proceed.\
It is a *situated move* in the sense [`A0`](../../axioms/README.md) draws - you reach for it when the work matches its trigger, unlike an axiom, which is in force whether you reach for it or not.

The layer exists because conduct is where good work and adequate work diverge invisibly.\
Two engineers can produce the same artifact by procedures of very different reliability, and the artifact will not tell you which.\
Without a named procedure the difference is detectable only after it has cost something.

---

## Territory

This set claims to cover **the procedural decisions inside a unit of engineering work** - the points at which how you proceed, rather than what you produce, determines whether the result can be trusted.

Those decisions fall at five moments in a unit of work, and the moments are the denominator.\
A gap is a moment with no procedure, or a recurring failure at a moment that no procedure guards.

| Moment | The decision | Members |
|---|---|---|
| **Entering** | how to begin work in an unfamiliar collection or system | [`M6`](../../methodology/M6-author-from-exemplar.md), [`M8`](../../methodology/M8-artifact-bootstrap.md) |
| **Committing to a design** | whether a design is anchored before it is built | [`M7`](../../methodology/M7-axiom-alignment-audit.md) |
| **Verifying** | what evidence is enough to believe the work is correct | [`M1`](../../methodology/M1-triangulated-review.md), [`M2`](../../methodology/M2-test-drive-docs-by-execution.md) |
| **Deciding what to keep** | what lands, what is cut, and how a cut is recorded | [`M3`](../../methodology/M3-default-reject-honest-yield.md), [`M5`](../../methodology/M5-anti-amnesia-deferral.md) |
| **After the fact** | how the record of past work is treated when policy changes | [`M4`](../../methodology/M4-frozen-history-rule.md) |

**Visible thinnesses, stated rather than filled.**\
*Committing to a design* holds one procedure and it is a heavyweight gate; there is nothing for a small design decision that does not warrant a full audit.\
*Handing over* - passing work in progress to another agent or a later session - has no procedure at all, though the corpus's reader starts cold by definition and handover is the moment that condition bites.\
Neither is a defect until something fails there; both are where to look first when something does.

## What belongs here, and what does not

The layer boundary is the object the rule acts on.

| If the rule governs | It belongs in | Because |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the process you follow | `methodology/` | the object is the conduct of work |
| the artifact you produce | [`style/`](../../style/README.md) | the object is the text, and a reader judges it without watching you work |
| the shape of a solution | [`patterns/`](../../patterns/README.md) | the object is a design, reusable across processes |
| a capability you execute | [`skills/`](../../skills/README.md) | the object is a procedure with inputs and outputs, invoked rather than followed |
| what must always hold | [`axioms/`](../../axioms/README.md) | it is not declinable, so it is not a move |

The discriminating question is what a reviewer would inspect to tell whether the rule was honoured.\
If they would read the output, it is style.\
If they would have to know how you arrived at it, it is methodology.

---

## What earns an entry

A procedure earns one when following it and not following it produce measurably different work, and the difference has actually been observed.\
A named practice that everyone already follows codifies nothing.\
A practice nobody has run is a proposal, not a methodology.

Prefer strengthening an existing entry to adding a neighbour.\
Two procedures differing only in the situation they name will be applied interchangeably, and the distinction they were minted for is lost on first use.

## How the procedures compose

Members cite each other, and the citations are not decorative: they form a small graph with a definite shape.

**Two primitives carry the set.**\
[`M1`](../../methodology/M1-triangulated-review.md) - require independent inputs before a verdict - and [`M3`](../../methodology/M3-default-reject-honest-yield.md) - reject by default and report honestly what was kept - are each cited by three other members.\
Most procedures here are a specialisation of one or the other: a particular kind of independence, or a particular discipline about what survives.

**One cluster is a single discipline seen three ways.**\
[`M3`](../../methodology/M3-default-reject-honest-yield.md), [`M4`](../../methodology/M4-frozen-history-rule.md) and [`M5`](../../methodology/M5-anti-amnesia-deferral.md) cite one another, and together they govern what happens to work that does not land: reject it deliberately, record why with a condition for its return, and never rewrite the record of it afterwards.\
Applying one without the others leaves a gap the others were covering - a cut with no revival trigger is forgotten, and a deferral whose record can later be rewritten was never durable.

**Two members are compound.**\
[`M7`](../../methodology/M7-axiom-alignment-audit.md) and [`M8`](../../methodology/M8-artifact-bootstrap.md) cite primitives and are cited by none.\
They are procedures assembled from others rather than new primitives, and adding a member should first ask whether it is a new primitive or a new composition of existing ones - the second is usually the right answer and the cheaper one.

**One tension, named rather than resolved.**\
The purpose above says a methodology is declinable.\
`M7` describes itself as a required gate that every extensive design *must* pass, which is closer to a standing commitment than to a situated move.\
Either `M7`'s trigger is narrow enough that declining it outside that trigger is the declinability, or `M7` is an axiom-shaped rule in a methodology's place.\
This charter does not settle which; it records that the two statements pull against each other, so the next reader does not have to rediscover it.

## Faults

Population faults - each is visible only across the set, and no single procedure can see it.\
A failure mode of one procedure belongs in that procedure.

- **The unenforceable procedure.** A sequence with no observable trace, so conformance can only be asserted. If nothing distinguishes a run that followed it, it is advice.
- **The style rule in process clothing.** A convention about the artifact filed here, where the people who write artifacts do not look for it.
- **The procedure that is really a skill.** A named capability with inputs and outputs, filed as a way of working. It belongs in `skills/`, where it can be invoked.
- **The ceremony.** A step retained because it is in the procedure, after the failure it guarded against became impossible. Procedures accrete; nothing prunes them unless the entry says what it is protecting against.
- **The orphaned primitive.** A procedure that composes with nothing and is cited by nothing. It may be load-bearing and simply unrecognised, or it may be a habit wearing a methodology's name; the graph cannot tell which, and the absence of edges is the prompt to ask.
- **The unguarded moment.** A recurring failure at a point in the work where no procedure applies. It is the methodology layer's characteristic gap, and it is invisible until the territory above is read against what actually went wrong.
