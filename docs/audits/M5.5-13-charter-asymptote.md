---

## The rulings

- **A charter is an asymptote.** It describes the set's ideal end state, independent of the current population, and the population is measured against it.
- **The charter states end state, scope and growth policy.** Purpose opens with the end state. Territory states scope and a growth policy - capped or uncapped, the altitude a member sits at, what members are balanced on.
- **Partitions and gaps belong to investigations.** A territory can be cut in several defensible ways, and missing dimensions cannot be listed before investigation. An investigation is a dated analysis; a later one may cut the set differently without the charter changing.
- **Gaps become backlog rows; the board weighs them.** A charter does not rank its own gaps.
- **A charter does not name its members.** Only the generated index lists them; a set spanning layers declares its members in a `members` list.

## Addendum - charters do not know their investigations

**Ruling.**\
A charter has no knowledge that it is being investigated: no link to an investigation, and no passage naming one.\
Investigations cite their charter; the dependency runs one way.

**Applied.**\
The investigation links and five passages naming investigations removed from the sixteen charters; `E4` states the one-way dependency.\
The eval export's exception for `docs/investigations/` reverted, since nothing in the product reaches into `docs/` any more.\
Two fixes found on the way: `S0`'s growth policy had drifted to one sub-set per medium and is restored to the second rule in any non-document medium; `E4` says why the end state comes first.

**Measured.**\
Run `noinv-regression`, ten suites on the corpus with no investigations visible: 138 / 150, against 187.34 / 188 for the same probes in `asym-regression`, where investigations were visible.\
The drop is in eleven probes that ask for a specific gap - `charter-claims` V3, V4, V10, `s0` Q5, `p0` Q5, `k0` Q4, `ar0` Q4, `c0` Q4, `e0` Q4, Q5, `sc0` Q4.\
In every case readers found the absence by reading the members and then labelled it a guess or not settled, because nothing in the corpus now says it is a known gap; the scoring caps a labelled guess at 1.\
Re-keyed to credit the finding (`noinv-rescored`), the same answers score 115 / 124: the cap still applies, because readers still hedge.\
After the `S0` fix, fresh readers (`noinv-02`): `s0` 13.33 / 14, `asymptote` **11.67 / 12**.

**Reading.**\
This is the ruling's intended effect, measured: a cold reader of the published corpus finds where members are absent but cannot learn which absences are known gaps, because known gaps live in the project's record - the backlog and the investigations - not in the product.\
Two of those probes, `charter-claims` V3 and V4, ask for moments of work that were a partition and now exist only in an investigation; they test content the ruling removed.

**Ruled.**\
The eleven probes are retired, each recorded with its question and reason in the evaluation README.\
`k0` Q5 - is there a skill for blind evaluation - is the same class and scored 1 in `noinv-rescored`; it was not in the eleven put to the director and is left in place.\
**Trigger: it scores below 2 again on unchanged text, then retire it on the same ruling.**
